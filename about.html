ABOUT HAMAX

Less clutter.
More control.

Hamax is a Windows desktop downloader built around a simple idea: downloading media should be clear, practical and easy to understand.

Our approach

Hamax brings URL analysis, quality selection, format selection, queue management and download controls together in one focused application.

Designed for everyday use

The interface is intentionally straightforward. You can choose the settings you want without having to navigate through complicated menus.

What Hamax supports

Current output options include MP4, MKV, WebM and MP3, with available quality and FPS choices depending on the media source.
